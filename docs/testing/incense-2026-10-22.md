## Confirmed defect and repair

With normally equipped real dex incense, Magic Room, Embargo and native Klutz still allowed the matching boost. The ordinary move preparation path read raw `item`/`held_item`, bypassing the effective-item resolver even though that resolver correctly reported the item suppressed. For example, the held suppressed Psychic/Surf/Energy Ball power remained 108 instead of the unheld 90; Rock remained 96 instead of 80.

`engine._apply_move_damage` now reads `EffectContext.item(owner)` before offensive item hooks. Suppression retains ownership and removes the boost; toggling Magic Room off restores it. Existing incense definitions, callback multipliers and suppression storage remain unchanged. The common consumer fix is protected by broader held-item/battle regressions.

A second fresh ordinary-command reproduction used native Sand Force Gigalith with legal Power Gem: clear-weather power was 80 both unheld and with Rock Incense. An ability power result skipped the held-item hook even when it was a no-op. The same consumer now applies ability power then the effective item modifier. New native cases verify clear weather (80 → 96) and sandstorm (80 → 104 → 124 with existing integer rounding), including sand checkpoint rebuilds. The additional native Unnerve Tyranitar target avoids automatic weather and residual HP damage confounds; its harmless Safeguard action is assigned fixture setup, not proof of target move learnability.

## Normal player routes and controls

Explicit disposable setup grants bag stock and creates two ordinary owners with canonical slot-3 native Mew, legal active moves, fixed genetic/EV/nature inputs and calculated HP/stats. `+inventory`/`+itemdex` discover each item. `+hold 3=<incense>` transfers one real bag copy to the saved holder. A production PvP BattleSession runs both normal `+attack` declarations, full turn execution, callbacks, PP spending and persistence. No successful effect or accuracy/damage result is substituted.

Matching held attacks increase power; nonmatching Swift matches the unheld baseline. Repeated matching attacks preserve the held copy and original shared move power. Magic Room/Embargo are applied by actual moves and remain effective after two saved-state rebuilds. Magic Room toggle ends suppression and restores power. A native Klutz Swoobat keeps Odd Incense while dealing unheld-baseline Psychic damage. Psychic on native Dark Umbreon is an expected immunity, not a mechanics failure.

Each item also survives two rebuilds while its matching action is waiting for the other player. Runtime dex item identity, saved held state and the eventual power/HP/PP result are checked. Normal `+hold 3=` returns the previous copy to the bag, and replacement returns the previous copy while activating only the new matching type. Ordinary Knock Off removes the holder item; empty saved item/checkpoint state remains empty through two rebuilds and cannot resurrect the boost. Attacks do not debit bag stock, alter placement or rewrite genetics/progression.

Initial failures were diagnosed before repair: eleven genuine suppression leaks and ten fixture-only compact-name comparisons. A fresh subset reproduced suppression defects; unit fixture import/default-Normal mistakes were corrected separately. The first suppression-only build passed 38 PostgreSQL cases, 20 engine regressions, 790 focused tests and two full orders before the later native-holder concern exposed ability/item composition. That runner was stopped and its evidence retained under `suppression-only-verification/`. The final expanded frozen runs above supersede those preliminary results and cover both confirmed repairs.
