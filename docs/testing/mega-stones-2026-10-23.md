Trace copies a real Huge Power effect, initializes state/handlers and retains it
through rebuild; reentry resets Trace and copies through normal entry. Weather
activation/reentry and opposing weather replacement use pre-form modified speed
and independent sides. Real doubles prove Lightning Rod redirection/immunity
and Healer's private cure. Ghost immunity, opposing Dark status immunity,
reflection once, actual trapped switch, flinch prevention/reaction, sleep,
guaranteed critical/accuracy and suppression controls accompany power effects.
Conditional nonqualifying moves, 1.2 conversion once, shared-dex immutability,
weather/no-weather, No Guard on either side/protection, and Technician 60/61 are
covered in focused consumers. Existing foundation tests retain canonical Fusion,
Hyper Training, doubles budgets, Tera, faint/revive, stale state and rollback.

Parental Bond resolves two separate hits with a .25 formula second hit, one PP,
per-hit contact/substitute and secondary/self boosts, stopping after contact
faint. Protect prevents both; eligible fixed damage remains fixed per hit.
Its bounded extra-hit class excludes status, existing multihit, spread, Struggle,
charge/recharge/future/Z/Max, OHKO, recoil/drain/self-switch/self-destruct and
custom Hit/AfterHit/damage-callback classes. These retain ordinary behavior.
No broad multi-hit or generation rewrite is implied. Startup (including internal
TypeError) and second-hit faults propagate to whole-turn rollback; clean retries
spend one source PP/use and do not replay prior damage or weather.
