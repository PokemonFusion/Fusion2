# Native Ultra Burst

Prepare a real owned Necrozma and Solgaleo or Lunala through their ordinary
acquisition routes. Teach/activate legitimate Photon Geyser on base Necrozma
before combining at a Pokémon Center. Existing cross-generation level50 access
remains valid. Keep Photon in an active slot different from the selected
temporary Sunsteel Strike/Moongeist Beam slot. Stored-only or displaced Photon
cannot work while combined; separate, prepare the active set and recombine.

Equip the reusable physical Ultranecrozium Z on that actual paired host. In
human Wild, Trainer or PvP singles/two-controller doubles, use `+ultra [actor]`.
Doubles require your current A1/A2 or B1/B2. Review the exact source/PP/native
terms and answer yes or no. A yes reserves the form for that actor's next
ordinary `+attack`; it grants no standalone action or free attack. Reopening
after reconnect reads fresh terms. Changed terms require a fresh preview.

Native, active Prism Armor is required before activation. Copied, replaced or
suppressed ability, Transform, Human Fusion, foreign/boxed/egg/fainted hosts,
raw fused/Ultra grants and stale pair/source proof cannot qualify. The physical
crystal follows the existing Z policy under Magic Room/Embargo; suppression
does not supply Prism Armor. No Ring, key, quest, payment or plot flag is used.

Activation installs only runtime Psychic/Dragon Ultra stats and real Neuroforce.
It retains damage, status/sleep, stages, move PP and earned progress. The saved
host stays paired and its companion stays frozen. One Ultra use per side is
separate from Z. Activation neither pays nor refills Photon, so Photon0 is
allowed if another selected ordinary move is legal. `+ultra [actor] cancel`
clears only an unspent declaration, including while its ordinary move is queued;
that move stays queued. A legal switch/item/flee also cancels an unspent request.

On a **later completed turn**, use `+zmove [actor] Photon Geyser=<target>` with
positive current Photon PP and an unused side Z budget. Light That Burns the
Sky is Psychic200, single target, inherently accurate and ignores breakable
target abilities. Its category compares actual stored Attack/Special Attack
with their raw stages, skipping modifier events; ties are Special. Normal type
immunity, prevention and quarter-damage Protect apply. A started signature pays
exactly one Photon PP and one Z use. Ordinary prevention does not pay either.
The signature never reinstalls a replaced or suppressed Neuroforce.

Switching retains Ultra for the battle. Normal reentry restores its native
Neuroforce; reconnect preserves current ability/suppression without replaying
Start. Fainting reverts runtime to the paired form atHP0 while the use stays
spent. Revival does not reactivate Ultra. End/abort restores the paired form
and current resources before owned sync. Ordinary field separation remains the
same two-row service; it does not recover an old snapshot over newer progress.
Four-controller Multi, Mega/Tera/Dynamax stacking and unreviewed signatures
remain outside this route.
